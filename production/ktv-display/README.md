# No. 7 KTV screen and mirror ball

Three original still posters generated through the existing Merouter adapter with **grok-imagine-image-2.0**. Exact prompts/settings are in the adjacent `*.recipe.json` files; output hashes are in `assets.json`. Private provider responses remain under ignored `production/service-runtime/ktv/`.

- Kabukicho nights: rain, neon and Tokyo nightlife.
- Velvet revue: adult cabaret ensemble in stage dresses.
- Midnight stage: adult singer, lounge dancers and mirror-ball atmosphere.

Runtime: 12-second poster rotation, 1.5-second crossfade, slow framing movement, manual next/pause controls. The artwork retains its aspect ratio. These are animated still images, not generated video.

TV: 2.18 m wide cabinet (previously 1.22 m), mounted against the imported wall at 2.12 m centre height.

Mirror ball: 0.54 m diameter, 392 instanced mirror facets, two spotlights, 64 moving additive light patches ray-anchored to room surfaces. This is a stylized real-time reflection effect, not ray-traced optical simulation. Reduced-motion preference freezes automatic movement; users can turn the effect off in audio/room settings. No new shadow maps are allocated for the effect.

No automated test suite was run for this change.
